Som jeg er - Homo / Bi / Trans i Kbh. Bymuseet, København.
04/07 - 27/09 - 2009.


Copenhagen gay, bisexual and transgender persons story is a journey from the dark areas of violence confetti Pride parades at the Town Hall Square. Literature, social and educational system's history. It is the heterosexual Copenhagen history. And then it is your and my history. The exhibition is in conjunction with World Outgames in 2009, taking place in Copenhagen from 25 July 2 August.

Dennis Agerblad participating in the exhibition with a red dress, the Faroese hat, red shoes, jewelry, photos and music.
